RIGELIA — Privacy policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026. Public URL: https://amonterocabrera.github.io/rigelia-game-privacy/privacy.en.html.

Controller and contact

Aristides Montero Cabrera, Dominican Republic. Support and privacy contact: inforigelia@gmail.com.

Game data

RIGELIA stores progress, marks, stars, errors, Atlas discoveries, activity dates, streaks, preferences and tutorial state on your device so you can play and resume offline. This version requires no account and has no active game synchronization with developer servers. It includes no advertising, purchases or remote analytics. Any future advertising integration will require reviewing this policy and app disclosures before activation.

Device features

Reminders, when available and enabled by you, use local notifications and scheduling settings. Voice feedback speaks fixed praise messages using a voice reported by the system as installed and available offline; it does not record you or request microphone access. Speech engine behavior also depends on your operating system. Android backup: the version 1.0 configuration disables application backup and excludes its data from cloud backup and device transfer rules. No progress synchronization or recovery from a developer server is offered. This policy does not guarantee the behavior of every manufacturer transfer tool or remove historical copies.

Support email

Selecting Support opens your email application with our address. RIGELIA does not automatically attach progress, screenshots, a subject or a message body. If you send a message, we receive your sender address, message and attachments to handle your request. Please avoid passwords, identity documents or unnecessary information. Email is processed through our mailbox provider, Gmail/Google, and your sending provider under their services and terms.

The support procedure provides for deleting support emails and attachments under our control 14 days after resolving the request, without retaining copies in a support database. Only anonymous counts of issue and resolution categories may be retained, without addresses, names, original text or identifying details. Deleting our copies does not delete yours or guarantee immediate removal of the provider's internal backups. This schedule is not implemented by the app: the controller applies it through daily mailbox review.

Links and third parties

Educational references open external websites only when selected. These websites, your email application and system services operate under their own policies. This version does not sell game progress or send it to advertisers. Included third-party library and resource notices can be read offline in Privacy and data, under Third-party notices.

The web version of this policy uses GitHub Pages. GitHub retains a visitor's IP for security, including visits made without a GitHub login. You can consult its privacy statement for its practices and contact options.

We do not add forms, analytics, tracking pixels or automatically loaded external resources to this page's files. This description does not guarantee the absence of cookies or logs managed by the hosting provider. Visiting the page does not give RIGELIA remote access to progress stored on your device. The 14-day support-email period does not apply to GitHub logs; their retention follows the provider's practices and is outside our control.

Retention and deletion

Local progress is retained so you can resume. Erasing the campaign deletes campaign progress, Atlas discoveries, streaks and calendar data while retaining settings, tutorial state and migration receipts that prevent accidental restoration of old data. It does not erase original data belonging to another Ionic version or historical or external copies. Erasing the campaign does not erase all application data. Do not rely on recovering progress after uninstalling or clearing application data; we offer no remote backup to restore it. Contact support about information you sent us; we have no remote access to progress held only on your device.

Audience and changes

The app is intended for teenagers aged 13 and above and adults, not children under 13. Content rating and requirements for minors will be checked for distribution markets. This document does not implement age verification. An updated date and policy will be published when the described practices change.
